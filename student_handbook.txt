Student Management System Knowledge Base

Student enrollment requires students to provide their student ID,

name, age, gender, department, GPA, and enrollment date.

Students can belong to different academic departments such as CSE

and ECE.

A student's GPA is measured on a scale from 0 to 10.

The Student Management System allows authorized users to create,

view, update, and delete student records.

The system can also answer questions about student information,

department counts, average GPA, and the student with the highest GPA.
